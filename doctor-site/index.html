⑥ Patient agreement & contract off-system signing

After the plan is shared, if the patient agrees to proceed they sign the treatment contract off the system. Record it here — this unlocks issuing the device to the lab.

⑦ Issue STA device spec → STA Lab ★ prescription = AMD legal anchor

Once the contract is signed, the STA device spec is issued to the Lab to manufacture. The AMD's written prescription is the legal anchor (AMD signs it); the AMD or a senior ATC can then issue the approved spec to the Lab.

Issuing the spec to the Lab: AMD or senior ATC. Signing the prescription: AMD only.

⑧ STA delivery — QC accept / reject

When the Lab delivers, the AMD/ATC inspects the STA. Payment stays held until accepted. If it doesn't meet spec, reject it — the Lab must remanufacture (no payment released).
