The treating AMD is the Doctor on Record — they own diagnosis & device design, issue the treatment plan and order STA creation, and may delegate spec prep to a senior ATC (clinical responsibility always stays with the AMD). AMD Taiwan HQ is managerial only (monitors SLAs) and does not issue clinical decisions. A specialist consult with Dr. Felix is optional, the AMD's choice.
